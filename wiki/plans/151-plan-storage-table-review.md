### Approved change — one AggregateChain command-progress row

1. **Status and problem.** Approved, implemented and verified on 2026-11-03. The former `admissionPosition(singleton, lastIndex)` and `finalizedPosition(singleton, lastIndex)` tables preserve two necessary facts, but their separate tables duplicate singleton identity within the same physical owner. The earlier admission finding established the need for its cursor without justifying that physical split; its retain-separate-table verdict is superseded.
2. **Agreed representation.** Replace both AggregateChain tables with `commandProgress(singleton, admittedThroughIndex, finalizedThroughIndex)`. All fields are nonnullable integers; `singleton` is the primary key and the supported row is slot 1. `admittedThroughIndex` is the highest allocated `aggregateIndex`; `finalizedThroughIndex` is the contiguous prefix for which AggregateChain has retained original command `outcome` and complete pending deliveries. Both fields start at zero and obey `0 <= finalizedThroughIndex <= admittedThroughIndex`. They remain separate lifecycle facts within one row.
3. **Initialization and writes.** `onRepoStartup` inserts `{ singleton: 1, admittedThroughIndex: 0, finalizedThroughIndex: 0 }` once, with conflict-do-nothing semantics. Repeated activation preserves both positions. After initialization, readers and writers require that row; do not reconstruct it from pruned commands. Admission updates only `admittedThroughIndex` in the transaction inserting the admitted command and producer checkpoint. `recordOriginalCommandOutcomeTx` updates only `finalizedThroughIndex` in the transaction retaining `outcome` and complete command/external-operation deliveries. Neither writer may replace the row with a zero or stale value for the other field. Preserve strict predecessor checks, exact-result retry handling, safe-integer exhaustion and batch rollback.
4. **Reads and pruning.** Direct System command-ID construction, execution-through bounds and missing-history detection read `admittedThroughIndex`. Replay availability, predecessor validation and canonical-history adoption read `finalizedThroughIndex`. Preserve existing admission backpressure and `canonicalHistory` as the separately retained release/boundary fact. History-prefix pruning and reconstruction preserve both progress fields. The row does not establish downstream dispatch acknowledgement, provider completion or physical Archive delivery.
5. **Scope and alternatives.** This approved change covers AggregateChain and its in-scope callers, existing checks and runtime documentation. ServiceChain retains its separately queued review; do not silently broaden this change. Reject the old two-table layout because it supplies no ownership, transaction or recovery boundary. Reject collapsing the two positions into one number, deriving them from pruned commands, or copying them into `canonicalHistory`, because their independently lagging meanings remain necessary. Add no generic owner switch, compatibility alias, migration, fallback decoder or replacement coordination mechanism.
6. **Storage boundary.** This fixed-schema cutover requires empty affected AggregateChain storage. Use fresh isolated disposable storage for verification and preserve all existing stores. This planning approval authorizes no reset, deployment, publication or consumer activation against incompatible retained storage.
