## Agreed architecture

1. ServiceChain retains immutable admitted input and the first original `commandOutcome`. Original delivery selects null outcomes in `serviceIndex` order, invokes its retained `serviceVersion`, validates the complete returned `{ commandId, serviceIndex, commandOutcome }`, and retains the exact outcome/time transactionally after predecessor validation. Its existing completion hook transfers durable recovery to replay, including an empty pending queue after interrupted wake-up.
2. ServiceVersionRepo owns authored resources, one compact `executionCheckpoint`, complete `serviceVersionChainOutbox` packets and original external-operation/command pending work. Remove permanent `executedCommands`, `modelOperations` and projection fanout ownership from this resource owner. Resource changes/public refusal, checkpoint, complete packet and permitted original external work commit atomically. Business savepoint rollback and outer infrastructure Causes remain intact.
3. ServiceVersionChain owns permanent release-local execution history plus complete independent pending actor-projection fanout payloads and subscriber cursors. Packets include `serviceIndex`, command identity/provenance, exact `commandOutcome`, `dispositionHash`, `executionStartedAt` and canonical encoded model operations retaining exact model versions and sparse operation indexes. Delete a publication packet only after durable identical retention; delete fanout payload only after every covered subscriber acknowledges. Never delete retained history to acknowledge delivery.
4. ServiceActorVersionRepo owns the authored resource graph, selection computation, `projectionState` and complete `projectedCommandOutbox`. Applying an execution page commits resources, selected deltas, checkpoint and pending publication together. It never evaluates contracts or produces original external work. Preserve canonical operation codecs and execution timestamps.
5. ServiceActorVersionChain owns permanent selected history, private completion, connection locks and sockets. It owns no materialized resource tables/checkpoints. It validates immutable projected publication, commits before acknowledgement/broadcast, and retains history after delivery. Refusal disclosure stays bound to exact actor definition, Node/session, full encoded claims and complete producer identity; ServiceSessionLock model filtering remains authoritative for snapshot, replay and live delivery.
6. Snapshot capture runs at ServiceActorVersionRepo: capture resources/checkpoint N synchronously, publish pending selected output through N, compare ServiceActorVersionChain history exactly at N, and recover only the private prefix through N outside SQL. Keep current wire snapshot/socket fields, claims and lock validation. Ordinary snapshots/queries/authorization read current committed local state; do not force pending originals or another source-tip replay.
7. ServiceVersionRepo activation arms existing publication/external recovery and awaits bounded finalized Chain replay through the existing `.catchup()`. A local checkpoint ahead of the finalized source is valid. ServiceActorVersionRepo activation pulls and enrolls with `.subscribe()`. Original N still catches up predecessors through N-1; explicit command-result waits remain. No source drain/SQL/execution permit is held across recovery/history/source RPCs. Do not await downstream publication or external requests as activation readiness.
8. Original duplicate execution checks the checkpoint before catch-up/declarations, rechecks under its execution permit before fresh resolution, and retains the transactional contiguity check. Validate bound owner/version/safe position and exact authoritative admitted input before fresh effects; recover duplicates from pending publication first, then exact ServiceVersionChain history. Missing/conflicting/unavailable history leaves Chain pending without reevaluation, time resampling, resource mutation or repeated external production. No local result cache/history fallback is added.
