# ZeroSpinner replay decisions and the dormant cart

Research date 2026-11-19. Sources are ZeroSpinner `fadbed53a`, Core’s self-hosted spike at `5b443c170d`, and the original design conversation. The self-hosted spike is pseudocode. This research inspected the Workerd fixtures and recorded evidence.

ZeroSpinner’s design lets B prepare its own Repo from earlier deployments’ saved results. B can also evaluate old commands with genuinely new owner rules. The deployment that introduced a release has no special role in that design. Its saved results can be a source, while the active deployment remains authoritative. The hosted decisions establish no requirement for app 9 to ask app 8 to rebuild a cart. The hosted prototype also provides no evidence that app 9 may evaluate as soon as app 8 returns an empty history. [Plan 029](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/wiki/plans/029-plan-hosted-deployments.md:8) · [Hosted proof of absence](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/packages/zerospinner/src/retainedHistoryWorker.ts:78)

## What we agreed in ZeroSpinner

- **Ownership and placement.** Keep one stable admission order per owner. Each deployment owns its SystemRepo, Aggregate and Service VersionRepos, VersionChains, and archives. Persist the executor assignment before dispatch and recover the original on that assignment. The discussion preferred one serving deployment per addressed release, with a move to the latest only once prepared and selected. The consolidated plan uses one active deployment for fresh work. It automatically activates the first ready deployment and makes later activation manual. The earlier browser-to-candidate experiment and suggestion to dispense with activation were design alternatives. [Original conversation](codex://threads/01a1e017-21a7-7b33-b402-cdd4e2f174e6) · [Plan 029](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/wiki/plans/029-plan-hosted-deployments.md:5)
- **Replay and retirement.** Restore the exact saved evaluation for the target owner release, including outcome and operation payloads, into the new deployment's history. Apply the model changes without repeating external effects or sending old commands again. A genuinely new owner release can evaluate historical inputs under its own rules. Search activated history newest to oldest and distinguish found, proven absent, and unavailable. C can recover a record from A even if B never copied it. Prepare Aggregate instances lazily. Sweep needed history and outstanding work before manually retiring a deployment. [Plan 029](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/wiki/plans/029-plan-hosted-deployments.md:10) · [Exact Aggregate restoration](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/packages/zerospinner/tests/retained-command.spec.ts:147) · [A/B/C lineage fixture](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/packages/zerospinner/tests/platform-lineage.spec.ts:85)
- **Readiness and divergence.** Check every Service release at a captured finalized boundary before activation. Check a lazy Aggregate when first needed and refuse its admission if unavailable or divergent. Report the first differing disposition and boundary to the author. An unchecked cart remains unchecked. The gate compares Success/Failure tags. Exact restoration still requires the full saved evaluation, and a matching disposition hash leaves equality of model state and operation payloads unproven. [Plan 029](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/wiki/plans/029-plan-hosted-deployments.md:9) · [Disposition hash inputs](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/packages/core/src/contracts/dispositionChainHash.ts#L5)

## What the hosted prototype demonstrates

For candidate **Service** preparation, the retained-history Worker first asks stable authority for committed output. It returns `absent` only with a complete attested activated lineage whose SystemSpecs all omit the requested release. A previously activated deployment that registered the release prevents this proof even when it has no output for a particular position. A candidate that was uploaded but never activated does not enter that lineage. Missing local rows, unreachable history, or incomplete catalog evidence yield `unavailable`. [Retained history Worker](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/packages/zerospinner/src/retainedHistoryWorker.ts:78)

The candidate Service Repo may evaluate locally after that release-wide proof, fenced to the captured authority generation and admission revision. For a genuinely new release, the prototype compares its disposition to the verified original addressed-release disposition through the active deployment. That comparison reference is recorded as a prototype tradeoff. Existing releases use verified active same-release history. The Workerd fixture covers missing catalog, unavailable or forged active evidence, matched and divergent dispositions, and stale epochs. Production history provenance, lazy Aggregate readiness, retirement and Cloud divergence reporting remain open under MOR-463. ## Verification of finalized replay history

**Agreed for the self-hosted spike.** The stable `AggregateChain` or `ServiceChain`
owns verification of original results. It checks that the reply from the
addressed owner release matches the admitted command and its position, records
the original outcome, and advances the finalized position in order. It serves
only the contiguous finalized prefix to replay consumers. [Aggregate finalization](../../packages/system-worker/src/AggregateChain/recordOriginalCommandOutcomeTx.ts#L45)
· [Aggregate replay page](../../packages/system-worker/src/AggregateChain/readReplayPage.ts#L29)
· [Service finalization](../../packages/system-worker/src/ServiceChain/recordOriginalCommandOutcomeTx.ts#L37)
· [Service replay page](../../packages/system-worker/src/ServiceChain/readReplayCommandsPage.ts#L24)

A receiving VersionRepo consumes that trusted stream in order. If `user@3`
finalizes `applyCoupon` at position 42 and `user@2` has no binding for it,
`user@2` checks local support, records an unsupported outcome with zero model
operations, and advances its own checkpoint. The stable Chain remains
responsible for verification of entry 42. The local unsupported outcome
belongs to `user@2`. The original `user@3` outcome remains available from
its retained output source, including after the Chain compacts its input prefix.

## Earlier exploration with System release pins

The example below describes the earlier design with several System Workers
carrying copies of the same Aggregate release. The current self-hosted spike
uses one execution Worker per Aggregate or Service release, which can be
selected directly by a browser session. The predecessor checkpoint in the
following section still belongs to the Aggregate ID and destination release.

The self-hosted spike calls for two pinned System releases that can issue real commands against the same cart. The cart-rules version identifies which rules produced a result. The app release identifies which Worker can provide its code and stored results. Existing Core replay looks up the evaluation for the **receiving cart-rules version** and Chain position, from the Repo’s pending publication outbox or that version’s VersionChain. If the input originally addressed those rules and the evaluation is missing, replay fails. If it addressed other rules and the receiving version has no saved evaluation, Core can evaluate it as parallel replay. That lookup currently assumes one Repo and VersionChain namespace for the key. A miss in app 9’s own copy cannot settle what app 8 already committed. [Self-hosted spike](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/wiki/research/self-hosted-system-releases.spike.ts#L27) · [Target-version lookup](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/readEvaluatedCommand/findEvaluatedCommand.ts#L34) · [Aggregate replay branch](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/replayCommands/replayCommand.ts#L40) · [Service replay branch](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/packages/system-worker/src/ServiceVersionRepo/replayCommands/replayCommand.ts#L38)

Take an old cart with actions at positions 1–41. App 8 introduces new cart rules and app 9 keeps that cart-rules version.

1. If any eligible source already evaluated a position under the new rules, app 9 restores that exact output. Check the receiving Repo’s preceding state and model compatibility. A committed result in a pending outbox counts before it reaches the VersionChain.
2. If app 8 shipped or was routable and this customer skipped it, the skip proves nothing about evaluation, which could have come from a different session, projection catch-up, or preparation. The hosted Service proof would also be unavailable if an activated app 8 declared the release, even assuming the cart was truly untouched. A never-activated hosted candidate is different because the attested activated lineage excludes it.
3. If the relevant historical input addressed older rules and no accepted evaluation under the new rules exists, the needed work is first-time replay under the new rules. App 9 is a possible evaluator if it has the target rules and the declarations needed to replay the retained inputs. It would need an ownership rule that remains sound when app 8 and app 9 both try to prepare the same cart. Two isolated Repos can each commit their replay and queue publication locally. A durable winner must be established before either divergent result can be used as shared history or as the basis for a new original. Core’s existing local SQLite transaction supplies no cross-Worker guarantee. [Spike replay and preconditions](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/wiki/research/self-hosted-system-releases.spike.ts#L66) · [Hosted absence rule](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/packages/zerospinner/src/retainedHistoryWorker.ts:83) · [Core materialization transaction](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/materializeCommandTx.ts#L16) · [Pending evaluation and local checkpoint](https://github.com/morgs32/zerospin/blob/ab78365ff40eb806059bdab9c539249ca2afb3bb/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/utils/retainEvaluatedCommandTx.ts#L19)

The open Core decision is who proves there is no accepted evaluation for a cart, rules version and position, and who can durably establish the first one. The resulting lookup must survive retries, compaction and Worker retirement. Core's adopted canonical history supplies admitted input for the compacted prefix. It can carry an outcome from a different owner release, so retain the original result locator separately from that input source. ZeroSpinner’s single active deployment and its release-wide Service proof leave this self-hosted decision open. ## Starting a new Aggregate release from an explicit predecessor

**Agreed behavior.** An explicitly updated Aggregate release can inherit its predecessor's committed state and evaluated history through one fixed starting checkpoint per Aggregate instance and destination release. Record the predecessor and the exact command position and ID. After that point the destination follows ordinary parallel replay and can diverge from the predecessor. Later Shop deployments carrying the same destination release restore that release's existing starting checkpoint and its subsequent saved results. The current materialization head continues advancing independently.

Shop 8 runs `user@1`. Shop 9 adds a support Actor and ships `user@2` with the same models and contracts. The author can explicitly choose to carry forward `user@1`'s committed state for each customer. In the proposed API, passing `user@1` as the previous release names a logical owner release. The stable side resolves the retained source for the same System instance, Aggregate name and Aggregate ID. Require the same versioned model and contract bindings for this first experiment, and persist the predecessor identity in the immutable release definition.

The inspected Core checkout exports `makeAggregateRelease`, `updateAggregateActorRelease` and `upgradeAggregateContractRelease`. The Aggregate constructor has no predecessor field. The Actor helper inherits omitted declaration fields. The Tic-tac-toe example declares its next owner release directly with `makeAggregateRelease(gameReleaseV1.definition, ...)`. That argument supplies the Aggregate definition. Parallel Aggregate releases currently start their own materialization and evaluate history under their own rules when they have no retained output. A predecessor snapshot would grant a new, explicit way to initialize a release from committed command-derived state. [SDK exports](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/sdk/src/index.ts:14) · [Aggregate constructor](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/core/src/aggregate/make/makeAggregateRelease.ts:45) · [Actor update](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/core/src/aggregateActor/updateAggregateActorRelease.ts:120) · [Tic-tac-toe V2](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/examples/tic-tac-toe/src/gameAggregateReleaseV2.ts:8) · [Current replay rule](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/sdk/skills/zerospin-parallel-models/SKILL.md:44)

1. **Capture the old state at a fixed position.** Read all of the `user@1` VersionRepo's model rows and its materialization checkpoint together. If it returns position 41, ensure the stable AggregateChain finalized through 41 and the predecessor's complete evaluated history is durable through that position. Authenticate the snapshot binding and contents. The disposition hash covers order, IDs and outcome tags. Verification of the snapshot's contents requires authenticated source evidence beyond that hash. Actor snapshots expose selected rows and are scoped to their view. [VersionRepo head](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/readAggregateMaterializationHead.ts:8) · [Actor snapshot](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/aggregateProjection/getSnapshot/getSnapshot.ts:88) · [Disposition hash](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/core/src/contracts/dispositionChainHash.ts:5)
2. **Make positions 1–41 readable and install the snapshot.** Start with exact copies in the destination VersionChain's archive-backed history. Preserve original command identities and their addressed releases. Stage publication before exposing the destination and keep the copy idempotent. A more compact design can explicitly adopt that immutable prefix from the predecessor, with lookup and retention rules. Install the model rows and position 41 together in one new Repo transaction. Keep the new Repo unavailable until both rows and history are ready. A cursor alone leaves retries and projections without evaluated commands, and a fresh VersionChain rejects an append starting at 42. [VersionChain append](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionChain/AggregateVersionChain.ts:454) · [Gap check](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionChain/validateEvaluatedCommandPublication.ts:88) · [Already materialized replay lookup](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/replayCommands/replayCommand.ts:28) · [Hosted passive history precedent](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospinner/packages/zerospinner/src/AggregateVersionRepo/PassiveAggregateVersionRepo.ts:35)
3. **Catch up before taking a new command.** If Shop 8 committed command 42 during the copy, Shop 9 replays 42 before it can take 43. After the inherited checkpoint, the updated release uses its own declarations on first replay under the existing parallel model. Its model state may diverge from the predecessor while both continue taking real commands on the stable Chain. Keep pending originals and outboxes with their existing owners, and carry forward completed results without repeating work. Reuse the existing Chain’s order and its pre-execution check through the preceding position. [Original finalization](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/finalizeCommand/finalizeCommand.ts:58) · [Passive restoration](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/packages/system-worker/src/AggregateVersionRepo/utils/restoreEvaluatedCommandTx.ts:27) · [Chain history and pending work](/Users/morgs32/GitHub/zerospin/wiki/adrs/025-canonical-published-history-handoff.md:23)

The proposed calls for an individual customer could look like this.

```ts
const source = stable.resolveAggregateRepo(aggregateId, userV1)
const snapshot = await source.captureModelRowsAndHead()
await stable.verifyFinalizedSnapshot(source, snapshot)
await userV2History.copyEvaluatedPrefix(source.history, snapshot.aggregateIndex)
await userV2Repo.installSnapshotTx(snapshot)
await userV2Repo.catchUpThrough(await aggregateChain.readFinalizedFrontier())
```

The smallest test of the inheritance rule can restore the predecessor's exact evaluated commands through 41, reusing the current restore transaction with explicit predecessor lookup. A full snapshot install would then save applying the old writes again. The bootstrap contract, exact captured snapshot evidence and cross-release continuation have not been implemented or tested.
